Research

A focused PhD study on forecasting medication utilisation during flood disasters.

The problem: Flood disasters can disrupt healthcare services and medicine supply, making medication planning more difficult.

The research: This study investigates forecasting approaches for cardiovascular medication utilisation during flood disasters in Malaysian public healthcare facilities.

The purpose: To explore how forecasting can support better medication planning and disaster preparedness.

Evidence

Understanding Existing Approaches

Reviewing existing evidence on forecasting medication utilisation during disasters and the approaches used across healthcare settings.

Forecasting

Developing Forecasting Approaches

Investigating machine learning and deep learning approaches for forecasting medication utilisation during flood events.

Validation

Assessing Generalisability

Assessing how forecasting approaches perform when applied beyond the original development setting.

Research status: Ongoing. Results and research materials will be released progressively as they become suitable for public dissemination.
