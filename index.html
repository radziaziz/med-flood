Forecasting Medication Demand

An ongoing PhD research project exploring machine learning, deep learning, and foundation models to improve medicine planning and disaster preparedness in Malaysian public healthcare facilities.

Research Overview & Objectives

Investigating how artificial intelligence, deep learning architectures, and time-series foundation models can enhance disaster-resilient medication planning, emergency logistics, and stock management.

Motivation: Flood disasters disrupt medicine supply chains and healthcare services, making it difficult to maintain adequate medication supplies. Accurate forecasting can support timely planning and improve disaster preparedness.

Objectives: Develop and evaluate machine learning models for forecasting cardiovascular medication utilisation during flood disasters to support evidence-based medicine inventory planning.

Expected Impact: Improve disaster preparedness through more accurate medication demand forecasting and support data-driven decision-making for medicine inventory management.

Phase 1

Systematic Review

Review current evidence on machine learning approaches for forecasting medication utilisation during disasters, including commonly used data sources, models, and evaluation methods.

Registered protocol: PROSPERO CRD42025648931
Completed Manuscript Under Peer Review
Phase 2

Model Development

Develop and compare machine learning models for forecasting cardiovascular medication utilisation using healthcare and flood-related data. The study evaluates model performance and robustness under flood conditions.

Active
Phase 3

External Validation

Assess how well the developed forecasting model performs on independent data to evaluate its generalisability and real-world applicability.
